# English Studio

## Register

product

## Users

Artur, a Russian-speaking BIM manager learning English for work and life in the US or Canada. Initial learning baseline is provisionally A2, with grammar gaps in past statements and questions. Desktop and phone use are equally important.

## Product Purpose

An online PWA for short English lessons, useful reading and video material, practice with AI tutors, and durable personal progress. Tutors should use the learner's recurring mistakes and professional interests.

## Brand Personality

Calm, modern, adult. The user explicitly chose a restrained learning interface and short sessions without cartoon mascots.

## Anti-references

No cartoon mascots or busy game presentation, per the chosen direction. Avoid a marketing landing page before the actual learning surface.

## Design Principles

- Start directly with a useful next lesson.
- Make corrections explainable and immediately actionable.
- Give work, BIM, IT and relocation their own learning contexts.
- Persist real results, never manufacture progress.
- Use free AI services within their quotas; never silently switch to paid services.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, readable Russian and English text, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts and plain error messages. No particular additional accommodation has been specified.
